# Strix roadmap

> **Last updated:** 2026-11-22 · **Change:** patch (Project site and brand manual: landing page
> restyled in EN/IT/DE/FR around the three living-document pillars, 4-step assessment workflow, and
> five integrated technical modules including the Plots showcase)

Strix is a tool for teachers of mathematics, science and engineering: calculation exercises with
units, open and multiple-choice questions, tests assembled from an archive of exercises, scores,
graph paper and CAD drawings driven by the calculations. It is a fork of
[WordCraft](https://github.com/storytold/wordcraft) and is built on its document editor.

## The document editor (WordCraft)

The word processor underneath (typing, formatting, styles, tables, references, review, file
formats, layout and pagination, localization of the editor) is WordCraft's, and its plan is
WordCraft's: see the **[WordCraft roadmap](https://github.com/storytold/wordcraft/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)**
and its [milestones](https://github.com/storytold/wordcraft/blob/main/docs/roadmap.md). Strix
takes that work by merging upstream ([`docs/upstream.md`](docs/upstream.md)); the parity
documents still in this repository (`docs/target-app-parity.md`, `docs/gaps.md`,
`docs/*-parity.md`) are the snapshot taken at the fork and are not kept up to date here.

## Strix's own features

What is done is listed in the [README](README.md#status-and-roadmap) and described in the feature
documents: [calculations](docs/calc.md), [exercises](docs/exercise.md),
[question modes](docs/question-modes.md), [master documents and tests](docs/master-documents.md),
[archive](docs/archive.md), [DSA formatting](docs/dsa.md), [graph paper](docs/graph-paper.md),
[CAD](docs/cad.md), [plots](docs/plot.md), [plane structures](docs/structures.md), [Word round trip](docs/word-round-trip.md).

The latest feature, **plane structures** (docs/structures.md), is done in its four stages: the
solver, the structure object and its commands; the Structures tab between Math and Teaching with
the Structure Editor and its tables; the working written after a structure as live calculations,
algebraic and following the structure; dimensions, stations and the full-page Structure Editor.
On top of them, a whole exercise on a structure (Structure Exercise) and the switch between a
technical report and an exercise (To Exercise / To Report). Next there: the working written with
the structure's parameters instead of their values.

**Next** (to be confirmed by the owner, gathered from the feature documents' Next sections):
systems of equations across regions and matrices (docs/calc.md); a results table per exercise,
checks against expected values and a summary per variant (docs/exercise.md); ordering questions
(docs/question-modes.md); driving
dimensions with geometric constraints in CAD (docs/cad.md).
